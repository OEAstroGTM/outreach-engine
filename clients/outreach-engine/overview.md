# Outreach Engine

## What we do

AI-powered outbound agency. We run cold outreach for B2B SaaS companies end-to-end: infrastructure, copy, sending, inbox management. Clients pay a small tech fee to keep the engine running plus performance fees tied to qualified meetings that show up and close.

## Pricing model

Performance-based. Small tech fee keeps the infrastructure running. Real money is made when qualified meetings happen and convert. We take on the risk with the client.

## What we actually do (operationally)

- Email infrastructure: Microsoft 365 mailboxes via Inboxing.com, fully warmed
- Copy: personalized cold sequences per persona and audience
- Sending: EmailBison (send and personal instances)
- Inbox management: MasterInbox for unified reply handling
- Research: Apollo for prospecting and enrichment
- Channels: email, phone, LinkedIn (multi-touch)
