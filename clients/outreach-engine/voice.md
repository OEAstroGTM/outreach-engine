# Voice

## Tone

Conversational, a little dubious, self-aware. Sounds like a smart person writing to one person, not a marketer writing to a list. The rep knows this might not land -- that honesty is what makes it land.

## Sentence style

Short. Direct. No fluff. One idea per sentence. Occasional one-liner paragraph that punches.

## What good looks like

- "Might be off base here, so feel free to ignore this."
- "Not the usual pitch."
- "Sent this before. Not going to pretend otherwise."
- "Pretty sure this went the same place as that one Slack message you meant to reply to and then just kept moving."
- "Guessing this landed somewhere between your LinkedIn requests and a newsletter you definitely meant to unsubscribe from six months ago."

## Rules

- No em dashes (ever)
- No "I hope this finds you well" or any version of it
- No exclamation points in cold outreach
- No buzzwords: synergy, leverage, streamline, game-changer, cutting-edge, innovative
- No Netflix jokes or "binge-watching" analogies -- screams mass email
- Avoid "schedule a call" -- use "quick look", "15 minutes", "worth a conversation"
- Spin syntax for sender titles: {{Growth Lead|Head of Outbound|Outbound Strategy Lead}} (sales) or {{Growth Lead|Head of Outbound|Demand Generation Lead}} (marketing)
- Spin syntax for brand: {{Outreach Engine|OE Growth Lab}}

## Email length

Step 1: 3-5 short paragraphs. Under 150 words.
Step 2 (nudge): 2-3 lines + links. Under 60 words.
Step 3 (authority question): 1 question + framing. Under 80 words.

## Subject lines

Always {FIRST_NAME}. Never a question. Never a subject that telegraphs it is a sales email.
