# Objections

## Common objections from replies and calls

### "We already have an SDR / sales team doing this"
Response angle: we are not replacing your SDR, we are filling their calendar. Most SDRs spend 60-70% of their time prospecting. We take that off their plate so they close instead of prospect.

### "We tried outbound before and it did not work"
Response angle: what did not work was usually the copy, the targeting, or running it without warming infrastructure. Ask what they tried. The answer usually reveals a fixable problem.

### "We are not ready / timing is not right"
Response angle: ask what would have to be true for timing to be right. Usually reveals a budget cycle or a headcount milestone. File for follow-up with a specific trigger.

### "How much does it cost?"
Response angle: small tech fee (ranges $2,000-$20,000 depending on scope) plus performance. We are likely cheaper than one SDR salary fully loaded. Send the cost comparison doc.

### "We only do inbound"
Response angle: inbound is a bet on algorithm stability. What happens when CPCs go up or the algorithm changes? Outbound is the channel you control.

### "Send me more information"
Response angle: send the one-pager and case studies, then follow up within 48 hours. "More info" is often a soft no -- treat it as a warm maybe and move fast.

## Patterns to watch

- "Not interested" with no reason = likely saw through the copy as mass outbound. Flag these sequences for copy review.
- Replies asking about pricing = strong signal. Always respond same day.
- Replies asking "how does this work?" = bottom-of-funnel. Book immediately, do not over-explain in email.
