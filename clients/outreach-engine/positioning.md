# Positioning

## Core angle

We take on the risk. Unlike agencies that charge retainers regardless of results, we only make real money when qualified meetings happen and convert. Small tech fee covers infrastructure. Everything else is performance.

## What makes us different

1. Performance pricing -- we eat what we kill
2. Full-stack outreach -- email + phone + LinkedIn so buyers get touched in whatever channel they actually respond to. Most teams only hit one
3. Speed to pipeline -- AskTuring went from payment to first qualified meeting in under 10 days
4. We handle everything -- clients do not need to hire an SDR, build sequences, or manage inboxes

## Messaging by persona

### Sales leaders
- Lead with: reps spending too much time finding people to close instead of closing
- Support with: 70-80% rep-sourced pipeline is where inconsistency lives
- CTA angle: what percentage of pipeline is rep-sourced vs. other channels?

### Marketing leaders
- Lead with: pipeline too dependent on ad spend -- unpredictable when budgets shift
- Support with: marketing gets blamed for lead quality when outbound is missing
- CTA angle: what is the split between inbound and outbound as pipeline sources?

## What to lead with in outbound

For net new contacts: pain-led opener, then performance pricing proof, then social proof (named clients + numbers)

For saved contacts: proof-first, not pain-first. These people have seen the pitch. Lead with a specific result from a client that looks like them. Skip the pain framing.

## What NOT to say

- Do not open with "I hope this finds you well"
- Do not use Netflix analogies or mass-email tropes -- signals mass outbound immediately
- Do not be vague about social proof -- name clients, name numbers
- Do not pitch "outbound as a service" generically -- tie it to their specific pipeline problem
- No em dashes
- No "synergy", "leverage", "streamline", "game-changer"

## Tone

Dubious. Slightly self-aware that this is a cold email. Not aggressive. Conversational. Short. The rep writing is smart and knows this might not land -- that honesty is disarming.
