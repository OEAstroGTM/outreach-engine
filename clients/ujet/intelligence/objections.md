# Ujet — Objections
> Source: UJET-Spiral-Campaign-Messaging-Paths · Feel → Felt → Found framework, phone & reply

## "We already have conversation analytics / QM tooling."
Fair — most teams we talk to **felt** the same, they'd already invested there. What they **found** is those tools report on categories someone defined last year, so anything new gets miscounted or missed. Spiral builds the categories from the conversations themselves and updates them as issues change. The 20 minutes is worth it to see the difference on your own data.

## "We don't have bandwidth for another implementation."
Understood — everyone we work with **felt** burned by six-month analytics rollouts. What they **found** with Spiral is there's no taxonomy project and no manual pre-work: it connects to what you already run and it's live in hours. The bandwidth ask is the demo, not the deployment.

## "Send me some information."
Happy to — and I will. Most people who asked us that **felt** a deck would tell them enough. What they **found** is the product only makes sense when it's answering a question about their volume. So I'll send the one-pager, and let's hold 20 minutes so you can ask it something real.

## "We're building this internally / in BI."
Respect that — several of our customers' teams **felt** the same and started down that road. What they **found** is BI answers "what happened" from structured fields; the why lives in the transcripts, and reading those at scale is the whole product. Worth comparing what your build covers against what this does out of the box.
