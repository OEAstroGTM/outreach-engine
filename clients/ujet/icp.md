# Ujet — ICP
> Last updated: 2026-07-30 · Source: UJET-Spiral-Campaign-Messaging-Paths

## Hard Qualifier
**10,000+ customer interactions per month.** Validated on the phone follow-up via the Situation question ("roughly how many customer interactions a month, and across which channels?").

## Four Persona Paths

### Path A — VP/Head of CX, VP Support/Customer Care
**Primary economic buyer.** Usually holds Authority and Need. Email probes the pain; phone probes Timeline (volume spikes, tool renewals, AI mandates) and Budget (existing analytics/QM spend Spiral displaces).

Angle: *You're measuring contact volume; you can't yet eliminate it, because the "why" is buried in the conversations themselves.*

### Path B — CX/Support Ops Leadership
**Day-to-day pain owner, champion builder.** Strong on Need, weak on Budget/Authority. Goal is a meeting that produces a champion who pulls in the Path A buyer. 2/4 BANT here = Need + Timeline; map the economic buyer via SPIN Situation questions. Meetings flagged for the AE as champion-development calls.

Angle: *Your team is drowning in the manual work of making sense of conversations — tagging, sampling, QA reads — and it still lags reality.*

### Path C — Exec Sponsor (COO / CIO / CMO)
**Holds Budget and Authority.** Converts less often but at higher quality. Business-outcome language only, no feature talk. Delegation replies are wins — route the delegate to Path A/B with a referral opener.

Angle: *You're being asked for AI with measurable ROI. This one pays back in days.*

### Path D — Product / Program / RevOps Champion
**Not the buyer; the wedge.** Meetings are champion-development calls; BANT capture happens on the second call with the CX buyer present.

Angle: *Your roadmap decisions rest on incomplete customer data — support has the raw truth and no way to hand it to you.*

## Trigger Signals (probed on the phone)
| Signal | BANT field | SPIN follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| "We're evaluating tools / renewal coming up" | Timeline | "What's driving the timing?" (Implication) |
| "We have budget for AI initiatives this year" | Budget | "What does a win need to show to keep that funding?" (Need-Payoff) |
| "I'd need to loop in my VP / IT" | Authority | "Who'd have to be convinced, and what convinces them?" |
| "Our tagging/QA can't keep up" | Need | "What's it costing you that it lags — churn, repeat contacts, headcount?" (Implication) |

## Cross-Path Routing
Referral replies from Step 2 / Step 3 / LI-4 enroll the referred persona in their matching path with a referral opener: *"{Referrer} suggested you're the right person for this."*
