# Ujet — Overview
> Last updated: 2026-07-30 · Source: UJET-Spiral-Campaign-Messaging-Paths

## What They Do
UJET sells **Spiral**, a customer-conversation analysis product for CX and support orgs. Spiral reads 100% of customer interactions (calls, chats, emails, surveys, reviews) and automatically surfaces the root causes driving support volume, churn risk, and operational cost — without manual tagging or a predefined taxonomy.

Core differentiator vs. incumbent tooling: categories are built from the conversations themselves and stay current as issues change, rather than reporting against a taxonomy someone defined last year.

## Go-to-Market Structure
- All outreach is **UJET-branded** — UJET email addresses, signatures, caller ID, and LinkedIn presence. Dynamic Sales is never named in prospect-facing messaging.
- Channels run **sequentially, not interleaved**: Email drip (~16 business days) → LinkedIn (4 touches, starts 5 business days after email exhaust) → phone reserved as the interest-triggered follow-up.
- **Conversion event:** Meeting Booked — HubSpot meeting type "Introduction / Discovery" on the assigned UJET AE's scheduling link.
- **Qualification frame:** BANT (2/4 verified fields = Deal/SQL). **Discovery frame:** SPIN, concentrated in the phone follow-up.
