# Ujet — Positioning
> Last updated: 2026-07-30 · Source: UJET-Spiral-Campaign-Messaging-Paths

## What We Lead With
The gap between measuring volume and understanding cause. Every path is a variation on: dashboards show the spike, nobody sees the why, because the why lives in the transcripts.

## Angles By Path
| Path | Core angle | Proposed subject spintax |
|---|---|---|
| A — VP/Head of CX | Measuring volume, can't eliminate it — the "why" is buried in conversations | `{contact drivers at {COMPANY}\|what's driving your volume\|the why behind contact volume}` |
| B — CX/Support Ops | Drowning in manual tagging/sampling/QA, still lagging reality | `{manual tagging at {COMPANY}\|keeping up with categorization\|your team's tagging backlog}` |
| C — Exec Sponsor | AI with measurable ROI, pays back in days | `{AI with a number attached\|support cost drivers\|avoidable contacts at {COMPANY}}` |
| D — Product/RevOps | Roadmap rests on incomplete customer data | `{what support knows that product doesn't\|roadmap evidence\|customer pain points, ranked}` |

Subject lines are proposals — **need outreach-team sign-off**.

## Safe Proof Points
- Analyzes **100% of interactions**
- **No manual tagging**, no taxonomy project
- **Live in hours**, connects to the existing stack

## Excluded From Cold Copy
"decision-grade," "AI Issue Hub," "unknown unknowns," the 95% and 98% figures, weasel words, condescending filler.

## Gated Proof
**Turo** (28s AHT reduction, 91% SLA improvement, 5% CSAT lift in three months; named COO, published case study) — Phase 2 LinkedIn and phone only, with attribution. **Pending kickoff approval**; not on the approved-proof list.

## Competitive Context
| Competitor type | Our counter |
|---|---|
| Conversation analytics / QM tooling | They report against categories defined last year — new issues get miscounted or missed. Spiral builds categories from the conversations and updates them as issues change. |
| Internal build / BI | BI answers "what happened" from structured fields. The why lives in transcripts; reading those at scale is the whole product. |
| Status quo (sampling + manual tags) | Sampling misses recurring issues; stale taxonomies miscount new ones. |
