# ICP — Intellectible

## Primary persona
**VP of BD / Capture Director / Director of Program and Contract Management** at a federal contractor actively pursuing US government contracts.

Also converting: BD Manager, President/CEO of small GovCon firms, VP of Cloud/IT Solutions at federal services companies.

## What they want (from actual call-booked replies)
> "We need a sophisticated filtering mechanism for solicitations that can also [analyze content]... We are less interested in proposal solutions because we have our own team with proprietary methods." — Daren Gerrish, 11 Series Energy

> "We are on a regular basis looking at opportunities on sam.gov. What would be the best way ahead to learn more about your solution?" — Marcel van Hilten, Maven Engineering Corp

> "Does your software read pdf drawings and extract the data for example material type, critical dimensions, finishes, etc.?" — Jesse Ornelas, Lark Industries

**The real job to be done**: They want to stop wasting hours on SAM.gov and portals to find relevant opportunities. They're NOT primarily looking for proposal writing help — they want smarter filtering and qualification.

## Company profile

### ✅ Confirmed in-ICP
- **Defense/aerospace companies** pursuing federal contracts (ITC Defense Corp, Valiant Solutions, JetMicro, MOCA Systems, Ewing Components AS9120B)
- **Energy services companies** with federal contracts (11 Series Energy)
- **Federal IT/cloud services** (VSolvit, NTSC Associates)
- **Engineering/construction firms** with federal work (Maven Engineering, Lark Industries, Moon Tree LLC SDVOSB)
- **Consulting firms** with program and contract management focus (Regency Consulting, Columbus Consulting International)
- **Small/disadvantaged businesses** with federal certifications: **8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB, Minority-Owned** companies — especially those with GSA Schedules (Office Design Group, Ewing Components)
- Industrial supply companies servicing government (GMS Industrial Supply)

### ❌ Confirmed out-of-ICP (do not target)
- **Marketing agencies** — higher reply rate but no meetings, wrong job
- **Pharmaceutical/life sciences** managing bid processes that aren't federal contracts
- **Office furniture/interior design dealers** (even if government buyers)
- **Universities and community colleges** — they receive government funding but don't pursue contracts
- **Housing authorities** — public sector buyers, not contractors
- **Finance titles** (Controllers, CFOs) — not the BD/Capture decision maker
- **IT staff/programmers** without BD role
- **Real estate companies** without a federal contracting division
- **Farms/agriculture**
- **Recruiting software companies**
- **UK organizations** — some meetings booked from UK campaigns, but low ROI vs US GovCon focus
- **Brazilian/Indian/South American companies** — appeared in interested leads but not core ICP

## Company size signal
- Small businesses with federal certifications convert well: 8(a), SDVOSB, MBE, AS9100/AS9120 certified
- Mid-size federal contractors ($100K–$1.5M contract award range per campaign targeting)
- Companies actively listed on SAM.gov as awardees in FY25/FY26

## Trigger signals
- Company has recent federal contract awards on SAM.gov (FY25 or FY26)
- BD team is manually sifting through SAM.gov / FPDS / portals
- Contract values in $100K–$1.5M range (validated campaign segmentation)
- Federal certifications: 8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB, AS9100/9120, NAICS codes in defense/IT/engineering/energy

## Geography
