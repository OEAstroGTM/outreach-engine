# ICP — Intellectible

## Primary persona
**VP of BD / Capture Director** at a federal contractor actively pursuing US government contracts.

Also seen: Director of Federal Sales, VP of Operations, Senior Contracts Manager.

**Confirmed misses (do not target)**:
- Controller / CFO / Finance titles — not the BD decision maker
- IT staff / programmers / consultants with no BD role
- Editorial or communications roles
- Housing authority staff (public sector buyers, not contractors)
- University / community college staff
- UK-based procurement organizations

## Company profile
- **Industry**: Defense, aerospace, IT services, energy, federal health, engineering — any company with an active federal contracting practice
- **Stage**: Companies in pursuit mode — they have a BD or capture team and are going after contracts, but feel their qualified pipeline is too thin relative to what's being published on SAM.gov
- **Size**: TBD from active campaigns — mix of small (SDVOSB) to mid-size contractors seen in pipeline

## Confirmed in-ICP company types (from campaign replies)
- Defense IT / technology companies (ITC Defense Corp, Valiant Solutions, JetMicro, MOCA Systems)
- SDVOSB architecture/engineering firms pursuing federal contracts (Moon Tree LLC)
- Defense communications (Mackay Communications)
- Companies where someone uses the word "contracts", "bids", "SAM.gov", or "pursuit" in their title/role

## Confirmed out-of-ICP (do not target)
- Pharmaceutical / life sciences companies managing bid processes that are not federal contracts
- Office furniture / interior design dealers (even if they sell to government)
- Real estate / construction companies with no federal contracting division
- Farms / agriculture companies
- Recruiting software companies
- Digital agencies
- UK-based organizations (different procurement system entirely)
- Public sector buyers (they post opportunities — they don't pursue them)

## Trigger signals
- Company has an active federal contracting division or BD team
- BD team is manually tracking SAM.gov / FPDS
- They're reviewing too many unqualified opportunities and missing the right ones
- Growth goal tied to federal contract wins
- Title contains: BD, Business Development, Capture, Contracts, Proposals, Federal Sales

## Recent pipeline examples (May 2026)

- INVOCON Inc. — aerospace / defense
- Monmouth Solutions — services
- haag-streit group — medical devices / federal health
- 11 Series Energy — energy services
