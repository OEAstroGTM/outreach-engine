# Intellectible — ICP

## Primary Persona
**VP of Business Development** or **Capture Director** at a federal contractor.

They manage a BD team that manually researches contract opportunities — spending hours in USASpending, GovTribe, or FPDS trying to build a pipeline. They're measured on qualified opportunities identified and win rate. They feel the pressure of FY deadlines and hate losing bids to competitors who had better intel earlier.

## Target Companies
- Federal contractors with $10M–$750M in annual revenue
- Active in defense, IT services, professional services, healthcare IT, energy, or federal construction
- Pursuing contracts in the $100K–$10M range (task orders, IDIQ BPAs, set-asides)
- Have an active BD/capture function — at least one person whose job is finding new business

## Geographic Signal
Any state but highest concentration historically: FL, NY, TX, CA, PA, IL

## Fiscal Cycle Signal
Companies entering their FY25 or FY26 planning window are most receptive. Reference FY timing in subject lines and first lines when possible.

## Trigger Signals (reach out when you see these)
- Posted a new contract bid or teaming agreement recently
- Hiring a BD manager or capture manager (LinkedIn job posts)
- Recently won a new IDIQ vehicle — they now need to find task orders to fill it
- Referenced GovTribe or similar tools in their job postings (they're already tool-aware)

## Who NOT to Target
- Pure commercial companies with no federal revenue
- Subs only — firms that don't prime their own contracts
- Companies below $5M revenue
- Federal agencies themselves (they're the customer, not the BD team)
