# Intellectible — Positioning

## Core Claim
Double your qualified pipeline without adding headcount.

## How to Lead
Don't open with features. Open with the problem: BD teams waste hours on manual contract research that leads to bad bids. Intellectible compresses that to minutes and surfaces the right opportunities automatically.

## Primary Angle — Competitor Displacement (best-performing)
Target prospects who are already using GovTribe, GovDash, or similar. They understand the category. The pitch: Intellectible gives you the same intel but with better contract-level specificity and AI-driven opportunity matching.

Use this angle when: you know they use a competitor tool, or they're a sophisticated GovCon firm that would have tried the category.

**Subject line pattern:** "alternative to [GovTribe/GovDash]" or "beyond GovTribe"

## Secondary Angle — Pipeline Math
Frame the ROI in terms they track: "If your team wins 1 more contract this year because you caught it earlier, what's that worth?" Most BD teams don't think about their research time as a cost — reframe it as one.

## Tertiary Angle — FY Cycle Urgency
GovCon BD is cyclical. Reference where they are in the FY cycle and why now is the right time to get their pipeline in order. Best used in Q3/Q4 of the federal fiscal year (spring/summer).

## Proof Point to Deploy
**Oceus Networks won a $520M contract.** This is the anchor case study. Use it in follow-up sequences after initial engagement, or when targeting larger firms ($50M+ federal revenue). Don't lead with it cold — it sounds like bragging. Earn it.

## What NOT to Do
- Don't lead with the product. Lead with the problem.
- Don't pitch "AI" as the value — it's a buzzword in GovCon. Say "contract intelligence" or "opportunity matching."
- Don't reference specific contract databases by name unless you know they use them.
- Don't make the ask a demo on the first email. Ask if it's relevant first.
