# Positioning — Intellectible

## Core positioning
Intellectible helps federal contractors double their qualified pipeline — not by chasing more opportunities, but by getting smarter about which ones to pursue.

## The problem they solve
BD teams at federal contractors waste cycles on opportunities that were never winnable. They either miss the right ones or spend too long qualifying the wrong ones. The result: thin pipeline, missed contract targets.

## The outcome they sell
More qualified opportunities reviewed, faster — without adding headcount. The Oceus case study is the proof point: doubled qualified opportunities reviewed per month, ~$520M in added qualified pipeline.

## Messaging angles (to develop)
- Efficiency: same BD team, more qualified opps reviewed
- Precision: stop chasing, start winning
- Pipeline math: if your close rate is fixed, the only lever is qualified pipeline volume
