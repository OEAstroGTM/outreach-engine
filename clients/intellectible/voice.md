# Voice — Intellectible

## Tone
Direct, peer-to-peer. Write like a smart colleague who's been in federal BD, not a vendor pitching software.

## Do
- Lead with the outcome, not the feature
- Use specific numbers when available (doubled, $520M, etc.)
- Talk about qualified pipeline, not "opportunities" in the abstract
- Sound like you understand the GovCon world — BD teams, capture, SAM.gov, pursuit decisions

## Never use
leverage, synergy, cutting-edge, revolutionize, transform, unlock, supercharge, game-changing, best-in-class, take it to the next level, streamline, empower, robust, seamless

## Email length
Short. GovCon BD people are busy. Get to the point in 3–4 sentences. One clear ask.
