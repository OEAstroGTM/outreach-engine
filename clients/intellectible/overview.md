# Intellectible

GovCon BD/capture platform for companies pursuing US federal government contracts. Helps BD and capture teams source, qualify, and pursue federal opportunities.

**Headline outcome**: Oceus doubled qualified federal opportunities reviewed each month, adding ~$520M in qualified pipeline.

## Product
- Federal opportunity discovery / sourcing (SAM.gov, FPDS, GSA, Contracts Finder, etc.)
- Qualification and scoring of opportunities against company capabilities — reads content inside attachments, not just surface-level NAICS codes
- AI "digital twin" of the client's capture process — mirrors their internal go/no-go logic
- Pipeline / pursuit tracking for BD teams
- Daily digest of qualified opportunities delivered each morning
- **Pricing Engine** — separate product line (AI-assisted RFQ to quote to order; seen in separate campaign)
