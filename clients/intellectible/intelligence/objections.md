# Objections — Intellectible

Running log of objections encountered in calls and email replies. Add dated entries as new patterns emerge.

Format: objection → what's actually behind it → how to handle it.

---

## "We handle it internally" *(first seen Jan–May 2026)*

**Objection**: "We are currently handling our bid process internally."

**What's behind it**: They have someone (or a team) manually doing what Intellectible does. They haven't quantified the cost of that approach or felt enough pain to consider a change.

**How to handle**: Acknowledge it — don't argue. Ask what their capacity looks like if the number of relevant opportunities doubles. The goal is to surface the volume problem, not challenge their current process.

---

## Flat no — "No interest" / "Not interested" *(first seen Nov 2025–May 2026)*

**Objection**: "No interest.", "Not interested at this time.", "We have no interest."

**What's behind it**: Usually one of three things — wrong timing (mid-pursuit cycle, budget locked), wrong person (not the BD/Capture decision maker), or genuinely not a federal contractor with an active pipeline problem.

**How to handle**: If timing, ask when to follow back up and set a reminder. If wrong person, ask for a referral to whoever owns BD/Capture before closing the thread. If ICP miss, move on.

---

## "Not interested at this time" — timing objection *(first seen Jan–May 2026)*

**Objection**: "Not interested at this time." (softer version of the flat no)

**What's behind it**: They may have a current solution, be mid-cycle on a big pursuit, or just not feel urgency. Different from a hard no — timing is the real issue.

**How to handle**: Respect it. Ask: "Totally fair — is there a better time in the next quarter?" Offer the calendar and move them to a 30/60-day follow-up. Don't push.

---

## Wrong person / contact no longer there *(ongoing)*

**Objection**: Prospect replies that they've left the company, are in the wrong department, or have no involvement in BD/contracts.

**What's behind it**: Targeting errors — reaching people who've left their roles, or non-BD titles (controllers, IT staff, editors, programmers, executive assistants). These are wasted touches.

**How to handle**: Before closing, ask: "Who on your team owns BD or Capture? Happy to reach out to them directly." Treat it as a lead-gen opportunity.

**ICP signals to fix**: Controller titles, IT/programmer titles, editorial roles, housing authority staff, university staff, UK-based organizations — all confirmed misses.

---

## Angry response — "I have no idea what you're talking about" *(May 2026)*

**Objection**: "I have no idea what you're talking about. Can you create an AI team to better qualify your leads?"

**What's behind it**: Hard ICP miss. This person is not in federal contracting at all. The anger is justified — the outreach was irrelevant to them.

**How to handle**: Don't respond defensively. Apologize briefly and close the loop. Use as a signal to tighten targeting — these responses indicate the list pulled companies that don't pursue federal contracts.

---

## "We use our portal / public tender process only" *(Jan–May 2026)*

**Objection**: "Requirements are publicly tendered... we are not able to register interest or send/receive tender information outside of the website." (UK-based procurement org)

**What's behind it**: Wrong geography + wrong org type. This is a public sector buyer, not a contractor pursuing opportunities.

**How to handle**: Remove from list. Flag UK-based and public sector buyer orgs as out-of-ICP.
