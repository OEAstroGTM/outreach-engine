## Confirmed converting hooks

### 1. "More proposals without more headcount"
**Hook**: "What if you could submit significantly more proposals this year without increasing headcount? Most bid teams are bottlenecked by the manual grind of reading through hundreds of opportunities posted daily just to find a fit."

**Why it works**: Removes the "we'd need to hire" objection before it comes up. Reframes the problem as leverage, not budget.

**Confirmed conversions**: Jason Fahy (Placecube, UK) replied "Yes, I would be interested." directly to this hook. Multiple other meetings booked after variants of this.

**Variants in use**:
- "What if you could submit significantly more proposals on Contracts Finder and FTS this year without increasing headcount?" (UK-adapted version)

---

### 2. SAM.gov manual grind
**Hook**: "curious if your team spends hours hunting through Sam.gov plus other portals by hand? / A lot of GovCons tell us finding the right contracts on Sam.gov eats up time and slows revenue."

**Why it works**: Names the specific daily pain without requiring the prospect to admit strategic failure. Marcel van Hilten (Maven Engineering) replied: "we are on a regular basis looking at opportunities on sam.gov."

**Confirmed conversions**: Marcel van Hilten (Maven Engineering Corp, BD Manager) explicitly confirmed SAM.gov pain and asked how to learn more.

---

### 3. Oceus / social proof
**Hook**: "Oceus doubled their federal qualified pipeline in 60 days using Intellectible. $520M in new bids surfaced, 95% less time qualifying."

**Why it works**: Three specific numbers (doubled, $520M, 95%, 60 days) make it credible. Daren Gerrish (11 Series Energy) booked a call after receiving a variant with this proof point.

**Note**: Also referenced as "HHS and other gov cons have already grew by millions" in some variants — Oceus is the stronger proof point (more specific).

---

### 4. "Find every contract you could win"
**Hook**: "if you could find every gov opportunity you could win and ones you might have missed that were winnable would that be of interest?"

**Why it works**: Pipeline math framing. Fear of leaving winnable contracts on the table.

**Confirmed**: Used in sequences that generated Daren Gerrish (11 Series Energy) and other meetings.

---

### 5. Digital twin / attachment analysis
**Hook**: "We build an AI that functions as a digital twin of your bid team, and it's the only one that analyzes the content inside every attachment to uncover winnable contracts that match your unique criteria."

**Why it works**: Differentiates from surface-level SAM.gov keyword search. Jesse Ornelas (Lark Industries CEO) asked: "Does your software read pdf drawings and extract the data for example material type, critical dimensions, finishes, etc.?" — showing this angle resonates with technical buyers.

---

### 6. Break-up / bump sequence
**Hook**: "Looks like my notes about Intellectible and finding right fit contracts automatically with AI haven't hit the mark, so I'll stop chasing you around the inbox."

**Why it works**: Pattern interrupt. Creates a moment of truth before the thread closes.

**Opt-out phrases in use**: "not today Satan", "abort mission", "red alert", "no bid"
