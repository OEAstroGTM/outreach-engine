# Winning Themes — Intellectible

What's resonating in outbound — subject lines, hooks, angles, proof points that drive replies. Update after every campaign.

Format: theme → why it works → example that performed.

## Active hooks in campaigns (May 2026)

The following angles are currently being used across MasterInbox campaigns. Not yet validated by reply volume — update this file as patterns emerge.

### 1. Manual grind / SAM.gov pain

**Hook**: "Is your team still manually sorting through bids just to figure out which ones are worth bidding on?" / "still sifting through SAM.gov..."

**Why it should work**: Speaks directly to the daily operational pain of BD teams. Doesn't require the prospect to admit a strategic failure — it's just describing their Tuesday.

**Variants in use**:
- "Curious if your team still manually sorts through tons of bids just to figure out which ones are worth bidding on"
- "If your team is still sifting through SAM.gov..."
- "GovCons keep telling us the real drag on revenue is hunting through SAM and portals to figure out which contracts are actually worth chasing"

**Status**: Generating replies (mostly negative/OOO so far) — need more data on positive reply rate

---

### 2. More proposals without more headcount

**Hook**: "What if you could submit significantly more proposals this year without increasing headcount?"

**Why it should work**: Removes the "we'd need to hire" objection before it comes up. BD leaders think in capacity constraints — this reframes the problem as a leverage question, not a budget question.

**Variants in use**:
- "What if you could submit significantly more proposals this year without increasing headcount? Most bid teams are bottlenecked by the manual grind of reading through hundreds of opportunities posted daily just to find a fit."

**Status**: In use across multiple campaigns — one confirmed "Not interested at this time" response, no confirmed positives yet from this specific hook alone

---

### 3. Oceus proof point

**Hook**: "Oceus doubled their federal qualified pipeline in 60 days using Intellectible. $520M in new bids surfaced, 95% less time qualifying."

**Why it works**: Three specific numbers (doubled, $520M, 95%, 60 days) make it credible. Big enough to matter, specific enough to be believable. Use in follow-ups after the initial hook lands.

**Status**: Consistent use in email sequences. Tim Webber (JetMicro) engaged after receiving this — wanted to book a call.

---

### 4. "Find every contract you could win"

**Hook**: "If you could find every gov opportunity you could win — and ones you might have missed that were winnable — would that be of interest?"

**Why it should work**: Pipeline math framing. Speaks to the fear of leaving money on the table, not just the pain of manual work.

**Status**: In use — one reply from Tom Weston (Valiant Solutions) who declined, but engaged enough to respond

---

### 5. Digital twin / AI that reads the attachments

**Hook**: "We build an AI that functions as a digital twin of your bid team, and it's the only one that analyzes the content inside every attachment"

**Why it should work**: Differentiator — most BD teams know SAM.gov search is shallow. The attachment angle speaks to a real gap.

**Status**: Seen in at least one campaign variant — not yet validated by positive responses

## Positive responses logged

### Tim Webber — JetMicro (jetmicro.com) — *Jan 2026*
- **Labels**: Information Request, Not Right Now
- **Response**: "I'll use your calendar to book something early next week."
- **Context**: Defense/government electronics company. He engaged after the follow-up sequence.
- **Signal**: Calendar CTA converted — the offer to book a call worked. The "not right now" label suggests timing friction, not disinterest.

### Will Thompson — BlitzRecruiter (blitzrecruiter.io) — *date TBD*
- **Labels**: Interested, 2nd call completed
- **Response**: "do you guys have a demo or website I can look at?"
- **Context**: Recruiting software company — likely NOT a federal contractor. ICP miss, but the hook worked well enough to generate interest and a second call.
- **Signal**: The demo/website ask suggests the hook resonated but the product wasn't immediately obvious. Worth noting: if even off-ICP targets are asking for demos, the hook is strong.
